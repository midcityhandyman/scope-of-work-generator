  • Gas range with a layout change: gas piping is excluded. Confirm the range stays put or add a licensed gas fitter line item.

Scope by trade

1. General conditions

Inclusions

  • Project management, scheduling of trades and one point of contact for the owner.
  • Permit application and scheduling of required inspections.

Allowances

ItemBasisAllowance
Permit feesflat estimate, billed at actual cost$400

2. Site protection and demolition

Inclusions

  • Floor protection from the entry door to the work area.
  • Dust barrier with zipper door at the work area.
  • Broom clean work area and a safe walkway at the end of each work day.

Exclusions

  • Demolition and debris removal (by owner, complete before the start date).

Assumptions

  • Owner demolition leaves framing, subfloor and rough utilities intact and usable.

3. Framing and carpentry

Inclusions

  • Reframe one partition or opening for the new layout, non load bearing only.
  • Wall blocking for upper cabinets where walls are open.

Exclusions

  • Removing or altering load bearing walls.

4. Plumbing

Inclusions

  • Relocate sink supply and drain up to 6 feet from the existing location.
  • Install sink, faucet and new shutoff valves.
  • Reconnect disposal and dishwasher.
  • Reconnect gas range in its existing location with new connector and shutoff.

Exclusions

  • Extending or relocating gas piping.

Allowances

ItemBasisAllowance
Sink and faucetone sink and one faucet$1,400

5. Electrical

Inclusions

  • GFCI protected countertop receptacles at current spacing where walls are open.
  • Dedicated circuits for dishwasher, disposal and microwave.
  • Under cabinet LED lighting on a wall switch.
  • One receptacle at the island fed through the floor.

Exclusions

  • Panel upgrades or service changes.

Assumptions

  • Existing panel has space and capacity for up to three new circuits.
  • Basement or crawlspace below allows a feed to the island.

6. HVAC and ventilation

Inclusions

  • New range hood duct to the exterior, up to 10 feet.

7. Insulation and drywall

Inclusions

  • Patch drywall at removed cabinets, backsplash and any opened walls, ready for paint.

8. Tile and waterproofing

Inclusions

  • Tile backsplash from countertop to upper cabinets, about 36 sq ft.

Allowances

ItemBasisAllowance

Inclusions

  • Supply and install new base and upper cabinets, about 24 linear feet.
  • Cabinet hardware, fillers, toe kicks and crown as selected.
  • Island base cabinets, about 6 linear feet, with finished back panel.
  • Template, fabricate and install quartz countertops, about 65.4 sq ft, with one sink cutout.

10. Appliances

Inclusions

  • Install owner supplied range, dishwasher and hood or microwave; set refrigerator in place.

Exclusions

  • Supplying appliances.

Assumptions

  • Appliances are on site, undamaged and sized to the cabinet plan before install day.

11. Flooring

Inclusions

  • Luxury vinyl plank flooring, about 180 sq ft, with transitions at doorways.

Exclusions

  • Subfloor replacement or leveling beyond minor patching.

Allowances

ItemBasisAllowance
Luxury vinyl plank material180 sq ft plus 10% waste$1,290

12. Paint, trim and finish

Inclusions

  • Prime and two finish coats on walls and ceiling.
  • Baseboard and casing reinstalled or matched.

13. Cleanup and closeout

Inclusions

  • Final construction clean of the work area.
  • Walkthrough with the owner and one punch list visit.
  • Product manuals and warranty information handed over at closeout.

General exclusions

  • Hidden conditions found after surfaces are opened (rot, mold, pests, unsafe wiring or plumbing). These are priced and approved by written change order before work continues.
  • Work outside the room named in this scope, including repairs to adjacent finishes not damaged by this work.
  • Moving, storing or protecting owner furniture and personal items.
  • Design, engineering or drawings beyond field layout.

General assumptions

  • Work is done on weekdays during normal working hours.
  • Owner provides water, power, a parking space and clear access to the work area.
  • All finish selections are final before materials are ordered.
  • Allowances cover material cost only unless stated. Selections over or under an allowance are settled by change order.
