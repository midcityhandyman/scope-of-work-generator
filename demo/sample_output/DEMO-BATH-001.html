Scope by trade

1. General conditions

Inclusions

  • Project management, scheduling of trades and one point of contact for the owner.
  • Permit application and scheduling of required inspections.

Exclusions

  • Testing for and abatement of lead paint or asbestos.

Assumptions

  • Home built before 1978: lead safe work practices apply to disturbed painted surfaces.

Allowances

ItemBasisAllowance
Permit feesflat estimate, billed at actual cost$250

2. Site protection and demolition

Inclusions

  • Floor protection from the entry door to the work area.
  • Dust barrier with zipper door at the work area.
  • Broom clean work area and a safe walkway at the end of each work day.
  • Debris haul off and disposal for all demolition listed in this scope.
  • Remove toilet, vanity, existing tub or shower, flooring and wet wall finishes.

3. Framing and carpentry

Inclusions

  • Blocking for vanity, accessories and future grab bars.
  • Frame shower curb and one recessed niche.

Exclusions

  • Moving walls, doors or windows.

4. Plumbing

Inclusions

  • Fixtures set in their existing locations.
  • New shutoff valves at each fixture.
  • Install toilet.
  • Install 1 vanity faucet(s) and drain(s).
  • New pressure balanced valve and trim at the tub or shower.
  • Install shower drain set to the new pan.

Exclusions

  • Relocating drains or supply lines.
  • Work on the main stack, water heater or water service.

Assumptions

  • Existing supply and drain lines beyond the room are serviceable.

Allowances

5. Electrical

Inclusions

  • 1 GFCI protected receptacle(s) at the vanity.
  • Install 1 vanity light fixture(s) on existing switching.
  • Electric floor warming mat under tile with thermostat on a dedicated circuit.

Exclusions

  • Panel upgrades or service changes.

Assumptions

  • Existing panel has space and capacity for one new circuit.

7. Insulation and drywall

Inclusions

  • Moisture resistant drywall on walls outside the wet area, finished ready for paint.
  • Cement backer board on tub or shower walls.

8. Tile and waterproofing

Inclusions

  • Waterproof membrane on wet walls, about 90 sq ft.
  • Wall tile at the tub or shower to ceiling height.
  • Waterproofed shower pan with flood test before tile.
  • Floor tile over uncoupling membrane, about 48 sq ft.

Exclusions

  • Natural stone, mosaic or patterned layouts unless priced separately.

Assumptions

  • Tile areas are estimated from the room size and confirmed by field measure.

10. Paint, trim and finish

Inclusions

  • Prime and two finish coats on walls and ceiling.
  • New baseboard, painted, and touch up of door casing.
  • Install mirror and up to five owner supplied accessories.

Exclusions

  • Replacing the entry door or window.

11. Cleanup and closeout

Inclusions

  • Final construction clean of the work area.
  • Walkthrough with the owner and one punch list visit.
  • Product manuals and warranty information handed over at closeout.

General exclusions

  • Hidden conditions found after surfaces are opened (rot, mold, pests, unsafe wiring or plumbing). These are priced and approved by written change order before work continues.
  • Work outside the room named in this scope, including repairs to adjacent finishes not damaged by this work.
  • Moving, storing or protecting owner furniture and personal items.
  • Design, engineering or drawings beyond field layout.

General assumptions

  • Work is done on weekdays during normal working hours.
  • Owner provides water, power, a parking space and clear access to the work area.
  • All finish selections are final before materials are ordered.
  • Allowances cover material cost only unless stated. Selections over or under an allowance are settled by change order.
