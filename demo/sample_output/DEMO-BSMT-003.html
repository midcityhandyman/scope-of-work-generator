Review before sending

  • Moisture history reported: get the source and the fix in writing before finishing walls.
  • Basement bathroom: confirm the under slab rough in exists. If not, slab work and an ejector pump must be added.
  • Bedroom without a new egress window: verify the existing opening meets egress size and sill height, or the room cannot be listed as a bedroom.

Scope by trade

1. General conditions

Inclusions

  • Project management, scheduling of trades and one point of contact for the owner.

Exclusions

  • Permit applications and fees (by owner).
  • Waterproofing, drainage, sump or foundation crack repair.

Assumptions

  • Owner has approved permits in hand before the start date and books inspections.
  • Owner has resolved past water entry. This scope does not warrant a dry basement.

2. Site protection and demolition

Inclusions

  • Floor protection from the entry door to the work area.
  • Dust barrier with zipper door at the work area.
  • Broom clean work area and a safe walkway at the end of each work day.
  • Debris haul off and disposal for all demolition listed in this scope.
  • Remove loose wall coverings, old shelving and abandoned framing in the finish area.

3. Egress window

No work is included under this trade.

Exclusions

  • New egress window or well.

Assumptions

  • An existing code compliant egress opening serves the bedroom.

4. Framing and carpentry

Inclusions

  • Perimeter walls framed with pressure treated bottom plates, held off the foundation.
  • Soffits around ducts, beams and drain lines.
  • Partition walls and door openings for: bedroom, bathroom.

Exclusions

  • Structural changes, post relocation or stair rebuilds.

Assumptions

  • Finished ceiling height meets the local minimum after framing and flooring.

5. Plumbing

Inclusions

  • Three piece bathroom: toilet, vanity and shower connected to existing rough in.

Exclusions

  • Cutting the slab, new under slab drains or a sewage ejector pump.

Assumptions

  • Under slab drain rough in for the bathroom already exists and is usable.

12. Cleanup and closeout

Inclusions

  • Final construction clean of the work area.
  • Walkthrough with the owner and one punch list visit.
  • Product manuals and warranty information handed over at closeout.

General exclusions

  • Hidden conditions found after surfaces are opened (rot, mold, pests, unsafe wiring or plumbing). These are priced and approved by written change order before work continues.
  • Work outside the room named in this scope, including repairs to adjacent finishes not damaged by this work.
  • Moving, storing or protecting owner furniture and personal items.
  • Design, engineering or drawings beyond field layout.

General assumptions

  • Work is done on weekdays during normal working hours.
  • Owner provides water, power, a parking space and clear access to the work area.
  • All finish selections are final before materials are ordered.
  • Allowances cover material cost only unless stated. Selections over or under an allowance are settled by change order.
